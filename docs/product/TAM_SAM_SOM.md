# TAM / SAM / SOM — AccessGuard

> Status: Draft v1 (2026-08) · Directional, bottom-up where possible. Validate before fundraising.

## 1. Bottom-Up Building Blocks

- **Sites needing compliance:** ~200M public websites (global); conservative **served** universe = orgs with >$1M revenue or legal exposure ≈ **2–3M organizations**.
- **Avg. contract (SaaS):** Starter $49/mo → Agency $399/mo → Enterprise $10k+/yr. Blend ≈ **$3–4k/org/yr** at scale.
- **Penetration ceilings by segment** drive the funnel below.

## 5. Sensitivity & Caveats

- **+ve trigger:** EAA enforcement expands → SAM/TAM grows ~2x rate.
- **-ve trigger:** overlay players converting to dev workflows → truncate SOM.
- Conversion assumptions above assume 14-day trial → 40–60% activation → 8–12% trial→paid → 90%+ logo retention, consistent with word-of-mouth + novel wedges.
