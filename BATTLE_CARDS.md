## Battle Card: vs. Deque / axe-core

### Deque Positioning
> "The industry standard for accessibility testing. axe-core powers 80% of automated detection. Enterprise platform adds reporting, guided testing, and services."

### When They Win
| Scenario | Why |
|----------|-----|
| **Enterprise procurement** | Established vendor; SOC 2; FedRAMP; 1000+ customers |
| **Guided manual testing** | Axe Auditor = best-in-class for semi-automated/guided tests |
| **Training/education** | Deque University = gold standard for a11y certification |
| **Mobile native** | Axe DevTools Mobile = mature iOS/Android testing |

### How We Win
| Our Advantage | Proof Point |
|---------------|-------------|
| **AI Remediation Built-In** | "Deque tells you what's broken. We give you the fix." 90%+ fix acceptance |
| **Dev-First UX** | VS Code inline fixes + GitHub auto-fix PRs + MCP Server. Deque = separate tools |
| **VPAT in 5 Min** | Deque: 40 hours manual. Us: 1-click with evidence links |
| **Legal Shield™** | Deque has no legal guarantee. We offer audit trail + AI responses + financial cap |
| **Outcome Pricing** | Deque: per-page/scan. Us: pay for fixes delivered |
| **Modern Stack** | Next.js 16, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind. Deque = legacy Java/Enterprise |

### Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Deque is the standard. axe-core is everywhere." | "We use axe-core too — it's our engine. We add the layer developers actually need: fixes, not just findings." |
| "We already have Deque licenses." | "Great — keep axe-core for scanning. Add AccessGuard for remediation. They're complementary, not competitive." |
| "Deque has FedRAMP." | "We're pursuing FedRAMP Moderate (Q2 2027). For now, our SOC 2 Type II + Legal Shield™ covers 95% of compliance needs." |
| "Deque University trains our team." | "We love Deque training. Use it. We solve the *after-training* problem: 'I know what's wrong, now how do I fix it fast?'" |

### Landmine Questions for Prospects
1. "How many hours does your team spend manually fixing violations each sprint?"
2. "What's your VPAT turnaround time for procurement requests?"
3. "How do you handle demand letters today — do you have counsel on retainer?"
4. "What percentage of your CI pipeline failures are accessibility-related?"

| Our Advantage | Proof Point |
